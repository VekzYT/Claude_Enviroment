## Where new ideas come from

1. Playing the game: every mechanic, item and gamerule is a candidate. The person on the team who plays is the best
   source we have.
2. Comments: "turn on X next" is the channel's call to action. Log every good one here with the commenter's name.
3. Trends: memes from the game's community (like the infinite-water one) make fast Shorts.
4. **Not** from asking an AI for "what if ideas". That's how the clones end up with identical channels.
