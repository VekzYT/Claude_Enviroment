Field Notes, FW26

The Philosophy

Built on contrast.

ZYVANO started with a single idea: that softness and severity aren't opposites, they're neighbors. Every piece pairs a brutal, oversized silhouette with a detail that's almost too delicate for it — an embroidered thorn, a hand-finished cord, a stitch line that curves when it could have run straight.

The emblem is a vine. Vines don't grow around obstacles because it's easy — they grow around them because that's the only way through. We design the same way: pressure in, precision out.
